Invoicelytics Telegram Bot: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Last Updated: April 02, 2025

General Questions
Q: What is Telegram Invoicing Bot?
A: It’s a simple Telegram bot that lets freelancers create, send, and track invoices right in their chats. Type /invoice [name] [amount] [description] to generate a PDF and a Stripe payment link—no bulky apps needed!

Q: How do I start using it?
A: Search for @InvoiceLyticsBot in Telegram (or click https://t.me/InvoiceLyticsBot), then send /start to get going. Easy!

Q: Is it free?
A: Yes, you get 3 invoices/month for free. Need more? Plans are $5/mo for 10 invoices or $15/mo for unlimited. Check /upgrade for details.

Using the Bot
Q: How do I create an invoice?
A: Send /invoice [name] [amount] [description]—like /invoice John Doe 50 logo design. You’ll get a PDF invoice and a payment link to share with your client.

Q: What’s the invoice ID for?
A: It’s a unique number (e.g., 1698765432) in the PDF filename. Use it with /status [invoice_id] to check if your client paid.

Q: Can I send the invoice outside Telegram?
A: Yep! Download the PDF or copy the payment link from the bot’s reply and send it via email, WhatsApp, or wherever.

Q: Why am I getting “Upgrade for more” after a few invoices?
A: The free tier limits you to 3 invoices/month. Send /upgrade to see paid plans and keep invoicing.

Payments
Q: How do payments work?
A: The bot creates a Stripe payment link for each invoice. Your client clicks it to pay with a card. You get the money in your Stripe account—set one up at stripe.com (#) if you haven’t!

Q: Are payments secure?
A: Yes, Stripe handles all payment processing with top-notch security. We don’t store card details—just the invoice info you provide.

Q: Why does the payment page ask for extra verification?
A: Some cards use 3D Secure (3DS) for added security. Your client might need to enter a code from their bank. It’s normal and keeps things safe.

Q: How do I know if my client paid?
A: For now, check /status [invoice_id]—it says “pending” until we add auto-updates. Or log into your Stripe Dashboard to see payments in real time.

Troubleshooting
Q: The bot isn’t responding—what’s up?
A: Double-check your command (e.g., /invoice John 50 needs a description). If it’s still quiet, email us at [eddyforgetty@gmail.com].

Q: I got an error message—what do I do?
A: If it says “Oops, try: /invoice [name] [amount] [description]”, fix your input (e.g., use a number for amount). Still stuck? Reach out to us.

Q: The payment link doesn’t work—help!
A: Test it yourself with Stripe’s test card (4242 4242 4242 4242, any future date/CVC). If it’s broken, let us know—we’re in test mode for now.
